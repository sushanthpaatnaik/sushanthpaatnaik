# 25 Contextual Background Prompts — /innovations

Generation prompts for the contextual background plates, derived from the
brief's §4–§6 art direction and §10 realism rule.

**These are BACKGROUND PLATES, not product shots.** The product cut-out is
composited on top by the site, so every prompt asks for an empty focal zone.
Do not let the generator put a product in the frame — that is what the
"no product, no packaging, no bottle" exclusion in each prompt is for.

---

## How to use

1. Paste `SHARED PREFIX` + the product's prompt + `SHARED SUFFIX` into your
   image tool.
2. Export **~1600×1000** (16:10). The site paints these at up to 718 CSS px
   wide, so 1600 gives DPR-2 headroom without waste.
3. Save as `<product>-context.webp`, quality ~82.
4. Send them back and they wire straight into the live system.

**One master or a pair?** The site tone-maps a single neutral master per theme,
so one file per product is usually enough. Generate a `-dark` variant only for
the ones flagged **PAIR** below — those have a light/dark intent too far apart
to tone-map safely.

---

## SHARED PREFIX

```
Photorealistic industrial photography background plate. Wide 16:10.
Shallow depth of field, subject plane softly out of focus. Subtle cinematic
lighting, controlled contrast, muted colour, premium editorial finish.
```

## SHARED SUFFIX

```
Composition: keep the centre and lower-centre of the frame open and
uncluttered — a clean, low-detail area where a product will be placed. Detail
and interest belong in the upper third and the edges. Background slightly
softer than a foreground object would be.

Negative: no text, no lettering, no signage, no watermarks, no logos, no
brand marks, no people, no faces, no hands, no product, no packaging, no
bottle, no jar, no container, no CGI look, no 3D render look, no illustration,
no neon, no lens flare, no oversaturated colour, no clutter in the lower half.
```

## Theme notes

Most of these tone-map cleanly from one master because they are already
mid-key industrial scenes. The two flagged **PAIR** — Aquamax and Aerophenter
— have bright outdoor/sky content that goes muddy when darkened, so generate
a second variant for those with the prompt amended to:

```
Night industrial setting, cinematic low-key lighting, deep shadow, controlled
rim light on metal edges, warm sodium or work-light accents against a dark
sky.
```

## After generation

Check each plate against three things before sending:

1. **Is the lower-centre clear?** The product sits there. If the generator put
   detail in the middle, regenerate — don't crop, it changes the composition.
2. **Any text or logos?** Generators reintroduce signage constantly. Reject
   anything with lettering.
3. **Does it read at card size?** Shrink it to 350px wide and look. Fine
   texture disappears at that scale — the scene needs large-scale structure to
   survive, which is exactly why generated flat textures failed here before.
